# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and engineering teams exploring CodeRabbit through short, self-directed demonstrations, on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors find a feature and understand its behavior through a focused video. Maintainers manage the entire feature hierarchy and video mapping in JSON without editing application code.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, approachable. Preserve the supplied video reference's nested navigation and dominant demonstration area. Per the maintainer's updated direction, use dark mode and the official CodeRabbit brand guide: white logotype, CR Orange accents, dark mauve surfaces, Geist interface text, and Hack technical labels.

## Design Principles

- Keep the recording readable and uncropped.
- Make selection and playback state obvious.
- Make features directly shareable and easy to reorganize.
- Provide useful states for pending recordings, failed loads, and empty content.
- Keep maintenance and deployment simple.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA with semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and a native mobile dialog. Require captions for prerecorded audio in every applicable video. The exception is a video that presents no information beyond existing text and is clearly labeled as a media alternative for that text, as defined by [WCAG 1.2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/captions-prerecorded.html). Verify final recordings and captions separately when supplied, before publication.
